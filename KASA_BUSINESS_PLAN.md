## WHY KASA

After analyzing 100 verticals across 10 Balkan countries with 100 parallel research agents, one opportunity passes the "toilet paper test" - something every business needs, every day, cannot avoid, and will pay for without thinking:

**Mandatory fiscal receipt compliance.**

Croatia's Fiscalization 2.0 requires every business to issue digitally-signed fiscal receipts transmitted to FINA in real-time. Every. Single. Transaction. Every. Single. Day. Fines: EUR 1,300-13,000.

Croatia already allows any internet-connected device (phone/tablet) as a fiscal device. Nobody has built the elegant app to make it work.

## THE PRODUCT

### Core Experience (30 seconds)
1. Open app → tap product or enter amount
2. App generates fiscally-compliant receipt, digitally signs it, transmits to FINA in real-time
3. Receipt sent to customer via SMS/email or printed on EUR 30 Bluetooth printer
4. Done. Legally compliant. 10 seconds.

### MVP Features
- **Fiscal receipt engine**: FINA XML integration, digital signing, real-time transmission
- **Product catalog**: Add products with prices, categories, photos
- **Beautiful receipt design**: Merchant branding, QR code, legal compliance
- **Bluetooth printer support**: EUR 30-50 thermal printers
- **Offline mode**: Queue transactions when WiFi drops, sync when back
- **Dashboard**: Today's revenue, top products, hourly trends
- **Multi-language**: Croatian + English (for tourist-facing businesses)

### Phase 2 Features
- Inventory tracking with low-stock alerts
- Employee management (multi-user)
- Customer database + loyalty
- B2B e-invoicing (Fiscalization 2.0 B2B mandate)
- Analytics and reporting
- Stripe card payment acceptance

### Phase 3 Features
- Tax optimization and reporting
- Accounting integration
- Multi-country expansion (Serbia SEF, Montenegro, N. Macedonia)
- API for third-party integrations
